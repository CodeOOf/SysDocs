1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose

This Hardware Design Document describes the physical components, electrical architecture, and mechanical design of the ADNS hardware platform.

1.2 Scope

The ADNS hardware consists of:
- Compute platform (NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin)
- Sensor suite (LiDAR, camera, IMU)
- Flight controller (Pixhawk 4)
- Power distribution system
- Communication radios
- Mechanical frame and mounting

3. COMPONENT SPECIFICATIONS

3.1 Compute Platform

Component:      NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB
CPU:            12-core ARM Cortex-A78AE @ 2.2 GHz
GPU:            2048-core NVIDIA Ampere GPU with 64 Tensor Cores
RAM:            64 GB LPDDR5
Storage:        256 GB NVMe SSD
Power:          15W - 60W (configurable)
Operating Temp: -25°C to +80°C
Dimensions:     100mm x 87mm x 35mm
Weight:         280g

Interfaces:
- 2x MIPI CSI-2 (camera)
- 1x Gigabit Ethernet (LiDAR)
- 4x USB 3.2 Gen 2
- 1x PCIe Gen4 x8
- UART, SPI, I2C, GPIO

3.2 LiDAR Sensor

Component:      Velodyne VLP-16 (Puck)
Technology:     Time-of-Flight laser ranging
Range:          100 meters
Accuracy:       ±3 cm
Field of View:  360° horizontal, 30° vertical (-15° to +15°)
Scan Rate:      5-20 Hz (configurable, using 10 Hz)
Points/Second:  ~300,000
Laser:          16 channels, 903 nm wavelength
Interface:      100Base-TX Ethernet
Power:          8W typical
Weight:         830g

3.3 Camera

Component:      Raspberry Pi HQ Camera (IMX477 sensor)
Sensor:         Sony IMX477R (12.3 MP)
Resolution:     4056 x 3040 pixels (using 1920x1080 for real-time)
Frame Rate:     30 FPS at 1920x1080
Pixel Size:     1.55 μm x 1.55 μm
Lens Mount:     C/CS-mount (using 6mm lens)
Interface:      MIPI CSI-2 (4-lane)
Power:          0.7W
Weight:         30g (camera) + 50g (lens)

3.4 Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU)

Component:      Bosch BMI088 (integrated in Pixhawk 4)
Gyroscope:      ±2000 °/s range, 16-bit resolution
Accelerometer:  ±24g range, 16-bit resolution
Gyro Noise:     0.014 °/s/√Hz
Accel Noise:    180 μg/√Hz
Sample Rate:    Up to 2 kHz (using 1 kHz)
Interface:      SPI
Power:          5 mW

3.5 Flight Controller

Component:      Holybro Pixhawk 4
Processor:      STM32F765 (32-bit ARM Cortex-M7, 216 MHz)
RAM:            512 KB
Flash:          2 MB
Sensors:        Triple redundant IMU, barometer, magnetometer
Interfaces:     8x PWM outputs, UART, I2C, CAN, USB
Firmware:       PX4 v1.14
Weight:         15g

3.6 Power System

Battery:        6S LiPo (6 cells, 22.2V nominal)
Capacity:       10,000 mAh
Max Discharge:  50C (500A burst)
Weight:         1,200g
Chemistry:      Lithium Polymer

Power Budget:
- Motors (4x):         Peak 800W (200W each), cruise 400W
- Compute (Jetson):    30W average, 60W peak
- LiDAR:               8W
- Camera:              1W
- Pixhawk:             2W
- Radio:               5W
- Total Average:       ~450W
- Total Peak:          ~880W

Flight Time Estimate:
- Energy available: 22.2V × 10Ah = 222 Wh
- Average consumption: 450W
- Theoretical time: 222 Wh / 450W = 29.6 minutes
- Practical (80% usable): ~24 minutes

3.7 Communication

Radio:          RFD900x Long Range Telemetry Modem
Frequency:      902-928 MHz (ISM band)
Transmit Power: 1W (30 dBm)
Range:          Up to 40 km (line of sight)
Data Rate:      250 kbps
Interface:      UART (MAVLink protocol)
Weight:         20g

Backup Radio:   2.4 GHz WiFi (802.11ac)
Purpose:        High-bandwidth (video, map downloads)
Range:          ~300m

4. ELECTRICAL DESIGN

4.1 Power Distribution

Battery (22.2V)
     │
     ├─▶ Power Module (current/voltage sensing)
     │
     ├─▶ ESCs (4x) ──▶ Motors (4x)
     │
     ├─▶ 12V Regulator ──▶ Jetson Orin (12V input)
     │
     ├─▶ 12V Regulator ──▶ LiDAR (12V input)
     │
     └─▶ 5V Regulator ──▶ Pixhawk, Camera, Radio

4.2 Wiring Harness

- Battery to Power Module: 10 AWG silicone wire
- Power Module to ESCs: 12 AWG silicone wire
- Signal wires: 22 AWG shielded
- Total harness weight: ~300g

4.3 Connectors

- Battery: XT90 connector
- ESCs: 3.5mm bullet connectors
- Sensors: JST-GH (Pixhawk ecosystem standard)

5. MECHANICAL DESIGN

5.1 Frame

Material:       Carbon fiber + 7075 aluminum
Configuration:  X-frame quadcopter
Wheelbase:      550mm (motor to motor diagonal)
Weight:         800g (frame + hardware)

Arms:           Carbon fiber tubes (25mm diameter, 2mm wall)
Center Plate:   5mm carbon fiber plate (top + bottom)
Standoffs:      Aluminum (M3 threaded)

5.2 Component Mounting

- Jetson Orin: Vibration-isolated mount (rubber dampers)
- LiDAR: Top-mounted (360° clear view)
- Camera: Forward-facing (±15° gimbal for pitch/roll compensation)
- Pixhawk: Center of frame (near CG), vibration-isolated
- Battery: Bottom-mounted (adjustable position for CG tuning)

5.4 Center of Gravity

Target CG: Geometric center of frame (motor-to-motor midpoint)
CG Tolerance: ±10mm horizontal, ±5mm vertical
Adjustment: Battery position slider (±30mm range)

6. THERMAL MANAGEMENT

6.1 Cooling Requirements

Jetson Orin:
- Passive heatsink (aluminum, 100g)
- Active cooling (40mm fan, 12V, 0.5W) for extended operation
- Thermal interface: Thermal pad (6 W/m·K)

LiDAR:
- Self-cooling (internal fan)
- Adequate ventilation (no enclosure blockage)

6.2 Operating Temperature

Ambient Range:   -10°C to +50°C
Component Limits:
- Jetson: -25°C to +80°C (junction temp < 95°C)
- LiDAR: -10°C to +60°C
- Battery: 0°C to +60°C (charge), -20°C to +60°C (discharge)

Strategy:
- Pre-heat battery in cold conditions (< 10°C)
- Active cooling (fan) if Jetson junction temp > 85°C

7. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

7.1 Ingress Protection

Rating Target: IP54 (dust protected, splash water resistant)

Protection Measures:
- Jetson: Conformal coating on PCB
- Camera: Lens hood, protective glass
- Connectors: Sealing gaskets (JST-GH includes seals)
- Vents: LiDAR only (self-sealed design)

7.2 Vibration Isolation

Critical Components:
- Jetson Orin: 4x rubber dampers (60 Shore A hardness)
- Pixhawk 4: Foam dampers (included)
- Camera: Gimbal isolation

Vibration Spec: MIL-STD-810G Method 514.7 Category 4 (rotary wing)
Expected Vibration: 5-20 Hz (rotor frequency), 100-300 Hz (motor harmonics)

================================================================================

8. SAFETY FEATURES

8.1 Electrical Safety

- Battery Management System (BMS): Cell balancing, over-current protection
- Fuses: 40A main fuse, 10A per regulator
- Voltage monitoring: Pixhawk monitors battery voltage
- Low voltage alarm: Triggered at 19.8V (3.3V/cell)

8.2 Mechanical Safety

- Propeller guards (optional, for indoor/training)
- Emergency cutoff switch (accessible on frame)
- Redundant motor mounting (lock nuts + thread lock)

8.3 Fail-Safe Features

- Watchdog timer: Jetson + Pixhawk
- Redundant IMUs: Triple redundancy in Pixhawk
- Emergency landing: Pixhawk can execute without Jetson

□ All connections secure
□ Propellers installed correctly (CCW/CW)

□ Camera image quality verified
□ Radio link established (ground station)
□ Pixhawk pre-arm checks passed
□ CG within tolerance
